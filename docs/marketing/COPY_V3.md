## 1. Punctuation rule (sitewide, non-negotiable)

**Banned in all visible copy: em dash (—) and en dash (–).** Also banned in SEO titles, metas, alt text, and schema strings. Hyphens in compound words ("high-intensity", "54-day") are fine.

Replacements, in order of preference:
1. **Period + new sentence** (default; matches the 54D voice: short, hard).
2. **Colon** when the second half defines or delivers the first.
3. **Comma** only when the clause is genuinely soft.

## 2. Studio display naming (no dashes)

**Rule: UI display name = `{City} · {Area}` with a middle dot (·), only when a city has more than one studio.** Prose = "our {Area} studio in Mexico City".

- Mexico City — Carso → **Mexico City · Carso**
- Mexico City — Santa Fe → **Mexico City · Santa Fe**
- Coral Gables, Hallandale, Bogotá: single names, unchanged.
- The middle dot is display-only. Never in slugs (`/studios/mexico-city-carso`), never in SEO titles (use "54D Mexico City Carso" or prose form).
